## Executive Summary

Maranatha has a functioning website with a structured product section and an active blog. The core SEO problems are:

1. All product and service H1s are single generic words with no keyword value
2. Multiple pages are missing meta descriptions
3. The site has no individual keyword-targeted pages for its primary revenue keywords (granite countertops birmingham, quartz countertops birmingham, etc.)
4. The brand name is inconsistently applied across the site
5. Two product pages have significant technical errors
6. No local SEO schema is present on the homepage
7. The primary homepage CTA sends users to a Google Form

### Homepage — maranatha.pro/

| Element | Current State | Assessment |
|---|---|---|
| Title | "Countertops in Birmingham, AL \| Custom Granite & Quartz" | Good keyword targeting, missing brand name |
| Meta Description | "Granite & quartz countertops in Birmingham, AL. Visit our showroom and upgrade your home with expert installation." | Too short, no urgency or differentiator |
| H1 | "Custom Countertops & Flooring in Birmingham, AL." | Best H1 on the site — correctly optimized |
| H2s | Products / Request an Appointment / Why Choose Us / Testimonials | Generic, no keyword value |
| Primary CTA | "Schedule a Consultation" → Google Form | Critical issue — sends users to external Google Form |
| Local signals | Birmingham AL mentioned | Pelham, Huntsville, and suburb areas not mentioned |
| Schema | Basic WebPage + BreadcrumbList | No LocalBusiness schema with NAP |

**Issues:**
- Sending users to a Google Form for the primary CTA is unprofessional and loses analytics tracking. A native contact form should replace it.
- No LocalBusiness schema means Google has no structured signal for the business name, address, phone, and hours.
- H2s have zero keyword relevance. "Why Choose Us" and "Request an Appointment" contribute nothing to SEO.

**Recommendations:**
- Replace Google Form CTA with a native lead form
- Add LocalBusiness JSON-LD schema with full NAP
- Rewrite H2s to include keywords (e.g., "Granite & Quartz Countertops in Birmingham, AL" / "Stone Flooring Installation")
- Add brand name to title tag: "Countertops in Birmingham, AL | Maranatha Stone and Floors"

## Brand Consistency Issue

The site uses "Maranatha Granite" throughout:
- Page titles append "- Maranatha Granite"
- Email domain: info@maranathagranite.com
- About page says "Maranatha Granite"

The actual business name is **Maranatha Stone and Floors**.

This inconsistency creates confusion for Google's entity understanding and may reduce trust with customers who search for the full brand name. All title tags, H1s, and schema markup should reflect the correct brand name.

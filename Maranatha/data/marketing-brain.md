# Marketing Brain — Maranatha Stone and Floors

This file defines how Claude should assist with marketing tasks for this company.

## Business Focus
Maranatha Stone and Floors operates primarily in Birmingham, Alabama, and surrounding areas, with growth interest in Huntsville.

Main services:
- Countertops (granite, quartz, marble, quartzite, porcelain, soapstone)
- Flooring

## Marketing Objectives

Primary goals:

1. Generate high-quality leads
2. Increase website traffic
3. Build local authority in Alabama
4. Expand visibility in Huntsville
5. Reach a long-term goal of approximately 50 leads per day

## Geographic SEO Priority

1. Birmingham AL
2. Surrounding Birmingham areas
3. Huntsville AL
4. Alabama statewide
5. Nearby states (Florida, Tennessee, Mississippi)

## Marketing Channels

Main channels to focus on:

- Google Search (SEO)
- Google Ads
- Website content
- Blog articles
- Local SEO
- Conversion optimization

Secondary channels:

- Instagram
- visual portfolio content

## Claude Responsibilities

Claude should assist with:

- SEO analysis
- blog article generation
- landing page copy
- Google Ads copy
- competitor analysis
- keyword research
- marketing reports
- content planning
- website improvement suggestions

## Content Direction

Content should focus on:

- real customer concerns
- material comparisons
- installation process
- durability
- project planning
- design considerations
- mistakes homeowners make
- differences between materials

Avoid generic marketing language.

Content should be:

- practical
- informative
- trustworthy
- conversion-oriented

## Local Authority Strategy

Content and marketing should reinforce Maranatha as:

- a trusted local expert
- a precision fabrication company
- a reliable installation team
- a professional stone specialist

## Lead Generation Focus

Marketing efforts should prioritize generating qualified inbound leads for countertop and flooring projects.

Calls to action should encourage:

- quote requests
- consultation requests
- project inquiries

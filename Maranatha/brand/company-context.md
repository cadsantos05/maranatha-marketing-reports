## Positioning Notes
Maranatha Stone and Floors should be positioned as a reliable, high-quality stone and flooring company focused on execution, precision, speed, and professional service. The brand should communicate trust, craftsmanship, and operational efficiency rather than generic promotional language.

## Geographic Priority
1. Birmingham, Alabama
2. Surrounding Birmingham areas
3. Huntsville, Alabama
4. Other relevant areas in Alabama
5. Secondary reach in Florida, Tennessee, and Mississippi

## Marketing Priorities
- Drive qualified inbound leads
- Improve organic visibility for countertop and flooring searches
- Strengthen local authority in Alabama
- Support paid traffic campaigns with strong landing pages and conversion-focused copy
- Build trust through content, SEO, and proof of quality

## Tone and Messaging Direction
The messaging should be professional, clear, credible, and conversion-oriented. Avoid vague or overly philosophical brand language. Focus on real customer concerns such as project quality, material selection, installation speed, precision, durability, and confidence in the process.

- Quality workmanship
- Accuracy and detail
- Reliable timelines
- Premium-looking results
- Practical guidance for homeowners and trade partners
- Trust before and after installation

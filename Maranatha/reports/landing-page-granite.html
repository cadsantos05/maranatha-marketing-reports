A countertop like no other.

Natural stone is a popular option for kitchen countertops, and there are many reasons why going with a natural stone is a great choice. There are several options for natural stone countertops, such as marble, granite and quartzite, and each type of material is unique in how it can serve its purpose. Natural stone is also unique because each slab is one of a kind, giving each project its own aesthetic with distinctive mineral patterns — so you'll have a countertop like no other.

The downside to natural stone materials is that they're sensitive to stains because of their porous composition. But with proper preventative measures like consistent cleaning and annual resealing, your countertops will maintain their beauty for years to come.

Granite: Hard, scratch-resistant, available in hundreds of colors. The most popular choice for kitchen countertops in Birmingham.

Quartzite: Naturally harder than granite. Scratch-resistant and ideal for high-traffic kitchens. Often confused with marble but far more durable.

Marble: Timeless elegance. Perfect for bathrooms, baking areas, and fireplace surrounds. Requires annual sealing.

Frequently Asked Questions.

How much do granite countertops cost in Birmingham?
Natural stone countertops typically start around $45 per square foot installed, depending on the stone selected and edge profile. We provide transparent, itemized quotes — no surprises.
How long does the process take?
From your first visit to installation day, most projects are complete in 2–3 weeks. The actual install typically takes 4–6 hours for a standard kitchen.
Is granite better than quartz?
They're different materials with different strengths. Granite is a natural stone — completely unique, heat-resistant, and long-lasting with simple care. Quartz is engineered. We carry both and can help you choose the right fit.
Do you offer financing?
Yes. We work with financing partners so you can get your dream countertops today and pay over time. Ask us about current options during your consultation.
Do I need to seal my granite countertops?
We apply a professional-grade sealer at installation. We recommend resealing once a year — a simple 15-minute process you can do yourself with a store-bought product.
Do you serve the entire Birmingham area?
Yes. We serve Birmingham, Hoover, Vestavia Hills, Mountain Brook, Homewood, Pelham, Chelsea, Alabaster, and surrounding communities throughout Jefferson and Shelby counties.

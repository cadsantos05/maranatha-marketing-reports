7 What To Do in Week 1 — Step-by-Step for Someone New to Google Ads

Follow these steps in order. Each step builds on the previous one. Plan for 3–4 hours of setup time total, spread across 2–3 sessions.

  • 1
    Create your Google Ads account
    Go to ads.google.com. Sign in with the business Google account (same one used for Google Business Profile if possible). Choose "Switch to Expert Mode" when prompted — this gives full control and avoids automatic Smart Campaign setups that waste budget. Set billing to credit card and set a monthly spending cap.
  • 2
    Install the Google Ads tag on your website
    In Google Ads: go to Tools → Measurement → Google Tag. Copy the global site tag (gtag.js) and paste it in the <head> of every page on maranatha.pro. If the site is on WordPress, use the "Insert Headers and Footers" plugin or Google Tag Manager. This tag is required before any conversions can be tracked.
  • 3
    Set up conversion tracking — phone calls + contact form
    In Google Ads: Tools → Conversions → + New Conversion. Create two: (1) "Phone call from ad" — set minimum call duration to 30 seconds so only real conversations count. (2) "Contact form submitted" — track the Thank You page after form submission. Without conversions, Google has no signal to optimize your bids later.
  • 4
    Create the first campaign: MSF | Granite | Search | Birmingham
    In Google Ads: Campaigns → + New Campaign → Sales → Search. Name: "MSF | Granite | Search | Birmingham". Bidding: Manual CPC (uncheck "Enhanced CPC" for now). Daily budget: $15. Location: Birmingham, AL metro area (use radius targeting: 30 miles from Pelham, AL 35124). Language: English. Network: Search only — uncheck Display Network and Search Partners initially.
  • 5
    Create the Ad Group: Granite Countertops — Birmingham
    Inside your new campaign: + New Ad Group. Name: "Granite Countertops — Birmingham". Default bid: $2.50 CPC to start (adjust after seeing average CPC data in first week). Add all keywords from Section 2 of this report — both exact match and phrase match. Do NOT add broad match keywords yet.
  • 6
    Add negative keywords (critical step — do this BEFORE launching)
    In the campaign, go to Keywords → Negative Keywords → + Add. Add all 27 words from the Negative Keywords section above (free, DIY, how to, repair, fix, crack, used, wholesale, cheap, discount, lowes, home depot, ikea, samples, rent, lease, jobs, hiring, career, salary, review, complaint, laminate, formica, tile, epoxy, paint). This prevents paying for clicks from people with zero intent to hire you. Adding negatives before launch saves 15–25% of budget from day one.
  • 7
    Create Ad A (Local + Premium + Free Estimate)
    In the ad group: + New Ad → Responsive Search Ad. Enter headlines and descriptions exactly as shown in Section 2 of this report. Set the Final URL to https://maranatha.pro. Pin Headline 1 to position 1 (so your main keyword always appears). Save and label it "Ad A — Local Premium."
  • 8
    Create Ad B (In-House Fabrication + No Middleman)
    Repeat the same process in the same ad group. Enter Ad B headlines and descriptions from Section 2. Label it "Ad B — In-House Fabrication." Having two ads in one ad group automatically sets up the A/B test — Google will rotate them and report performance separately for each.
  • 9
    Set ad rotation to "Rotate indefinitely"
    In Campaign Settings → Additional Settings → Ad Rotation → select "Rotate indefinitely." This tells Google to show both ads equally, instead of automatically favoring one before you have enough data. You want to measure them fairly for at least 4 weeks before making decisions.
  • 10
    Add all extensions (sitelinks, callouts, call extension)
    In the campaign: go to Ads & Extensions → Extensions → + New Extension. Add: (1) Call extension: 205.663.0400, schedule Mon–Sat 8am–6pm. (2) Sitelink extensions: 4 links as listed in Section 2 for each ad. (3) Callout extensions: 4 callouts as listed. Extensions are free — they increase your ad size and CTR at no extra cost.
  • 11
    Review and publish — then check back in 24–48 hours
    Do a final review: confirm billing is active, location targeting is set correctly, conversions are tracking, and both ads are approved (status: Eligible). After 48 hours, check: Are ads showing? Are you getting impressions? Review the Search Terms report to see what actual queries triggered your ads. Add anything irrelevant to negative keywords immediately.
  • 12
    Set a weekly calendar reminder to review performance
    Every Monday morning, spend 20 minutes reviewing: CTR, clicks, conversions, and search terms. Do not make major changes (pausing ads, changing bids) until you have at least 2 weeks of data. Google's algorithm needs time to learn. Resist the urge to optimize daily — give data time to accumulate.
Pro tip for Week 1: The single most important thing you can do in the first week is verify that conversions are tracking correctly. Call your own ad (from a different phone), submit the contact form, and confirm the conversion appears in Google Ads within 24 hours. If conversions don't track, all future optimization decisions will be based on incomplete data.

⚠️ Key Rules — Meta Ads

Use Advantage+ Audience — Enter the prompt above as a suggestion, not a hard restriction. Meta's algorithm will find your best buyers faster this way.

Respond to every lead within 2 hours — Meta leads go cold fast. Call the number within 2 hours of form submission. A quick response is the #1 factor in closing Meta leads.

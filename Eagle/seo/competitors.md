**Services:**

- Custom hoods, bookcases, shelving

- In-house design + delivery + installation

**Team:** In-house designers, delivery, and installers

**Differentiators they claim:**
- 40 years local history — deep brand recognition

### Eagle's Differentiation Opportunities
1. **Construction transparency** — be the only competitor that openly details plywood box, dovetail drawers, soft-close specs. Others sell aesthetics; Eagle sells engineering.
2. **Content strategy** — none of the local competitors appear to blog consistently. Eagle can own educational search terms.
3. **Multi-market** — Birmingham + Huntsville + Gulf Coast. Most competitors are Birmingham-only.
4. **Modern digital presence** — new site, modern design vs. legacy sites.
5. **Precision positioning** — "Structure-first" cabinetry is an unclaimed position in this market.

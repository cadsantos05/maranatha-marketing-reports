## Product Knowledge

### Door Styles Available
- **Shaker** — most specified style; 9 finishes including White, Antique White, Dove, Grey, Ivy, Navy, Black, Honey, Cinder
- **Oxford** — 4 finishes: White, Sage, Toffee, Mist
- **Haven** — 2 finishes: Dune, Ember
- **Edgeline** — White (clean, contemporary)
- **Casselberry** — Antique White, Saddle (more traditional)

### Construction Specs (use in content and ads)
- 1/2" plywood box (UV-coated exterior and natural interior)
- 1/2" plywood full-height back panel
- 3/4" plywood shelves with edge banding
- 3/4" x 1-3/4" hardwood face frame
- 5/8" dovetail drawer box construction
- Under-mount full-extension soft-close drawer glides
- Concealed European hinges with soft-close (6-way adjustable)
- Framed cabinet with full overlay doors and drawers

### Key Selling Points from Product Specs
- Plywood throughout (box, back, shelves) — not particle board
- Dovetail drawer construction — engineering-grade joinery
- Soft-close standard on both drawers and doors
- Full overlay = cleaner, more contemporary look
- Hardwood face frame = added front-plane rigidity
- Wide finish range: from classic white to navy, black, warm tones, natural

## Content Direction

Content should focus on:
- Cabinet construction quality (plywood vs particle board, dovetail joints, framed vs frameless)
- Installation precision and what poor installation causes
- Hardware quality (soft-close, concealed hinges, full-extension glides)
- Design decisions (door profiles, two-tone, ceiling-height cabinets, wood hoods)
- Storage efficiency and planning
- Consultation and planning process
- Mistakes homeowners make when buying cabinets
- Material and finish trends (2026: warm tones, wood grain, skinny shaker)
- Room-by-room applications (kitchen, bathroom, closet, pantry, living room)

Avoid:
- Generic "dream kitchen" or "beautiful spaces" language
- Trend-chasing without structural context
- Vague promotional claims

Content should be:
- Technical and credible
- Practical and informative
- Conversion-oriented
- Consistent with the precision/structure-first brand voice

## Local Authority Strategy

Content and marketing should reinforce Eagle as:
- A precision cabinetry company (not just a cabinet supplier)
- A structural-first approach to kitchen and bathroom design
- A reliable partner for homeowners and builders in Alabama and Gulf Coast
- A company whose quality starts at the box, joint, and installation — not the finish

## Lead Generation Focus

Primary CTA: Request a Free In-Home Consultation
Secondary CTA: View Projects / Gallery

Marketing efforts should prioritize generating qualified consultation requests for kitchen, bathroom, and whole-home cabinet projects.

## Google Ads Strategy

- Budget: TBD

**Campaign 2: Huntsville Kitchen Cabinets**
- Target: Huntsville + Madison county area
- Budget: TBD
- Keywords: kitchen cabinets Huntsville AL, custom cabinets Huntsville, cabinet company Huntsville
- Landing page: /huntsville/
- CTA: Request Free Consultation

**Campaign 3: Gulf Coast / Florida**
- Target: Santa Rosa + Pensacola area
- Budget: TBD (lower priority)
- Keywords: kitchen cabinets Santa Rosa FL, custom cabinets Pensacola
- Landing page: /santa-rosa/

### Ad Copy Direction
- Lead with precision and quality signals (not just "beautiful cabinets")
- Mention free in-home consultation in headline or description
- Use location in headlines where possible
- Reference plywood construction, dovetail drawers as differentiators in extensions

## Meta Ads Strategy

### Audience Targets
- Homeowners 35–65 in Birmingham + Huntsville metro
- Interest: home remodeling, interior design, home improvement
- Retargeting: website visitors who didn't convert
- Lookalike: based on past consultation requests (once data builds up)

### Ad Formats
- Carousel: before/after project photos
- Reels: repurpose from Instagram content calendar (Airtable)
- Single image: product feature ads (door profiles, finishes)

### CTAs
- Primary: "Request a Free Consultation"
- Secondary: "See Our Projects"

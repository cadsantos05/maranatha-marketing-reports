### Construction Specifications
- **Box:** 1/2" plywood with matching UV-coated exterior
- **Interior:** UV-coated natural finish
- **Back panel:** 1/2" plywood full height
- **Shelf:** 3/4" plywood with front edge banding
- **Face frame:** 3/4" x 1-3/4" hardwood
- **Door construction:** Five-piece door
- **Drawer box:** 5/8" dovetail construction
- **Drawer glides:** Under-mount full-extension soft-close
- **Hinges:** Concealed European-style with soft-close, 6-way adjustable
- **Cabinet system:** Framed with full overlay

### Cabinet Types
Base cabinets, wall cabinets, tall cabinets, vanity cabinets

## Brand Positioning
Eagle Cabinetry is positioned as a precision-focused, structure-first custom cabinet company. The brand communicates engineering quality, long-term performance, and technical expertise — not just aesthetics. Messaging avoids generic "beautiful kitchens" language and focuses on structural decisions, material quality, and installation accuracy that protect durability over time.

## Tone and Messaging Direction
Professional, technical, credible. Speaks to informed homeowners and trade partners. Avoids trend-chasing language. Leads with structure, precision, and performance. Every message reinforces that quality starts before the finish — at the box, the joint, and the installation.
